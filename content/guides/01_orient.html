The map is not a pipeline

The atlas distinguishes decisions that lead toward an enacted budget from three simultaneous execution chains: legal authority, substantiation of a claim, and cash. Budget registers, journals, property records and control certifications are created when the relevant events occur, rather than at the end of payment.

Read the whole process in the big-picture reference. Then compare the institutional map with the map of exchanges: one diagram groups agencies by mandate, while the other distinguishes the transfer of documents, money, and information.

Three questions to carry forward: Does an arrow represent legal power, a document, data, or money? Is the process required by rule, or an observed technical interface? What would demonstrate the arrow is wrong?
