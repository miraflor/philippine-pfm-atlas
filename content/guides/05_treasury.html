The government's cash system is not identical to its budget system. Receipts, borrowing, liquidity management and settlement form a second operational view.

Incoming public money

Taxes, customs duties, agency income and financing proceeds reach relevant collection arrangements and Treasury accounts through different procedures. Their supporting records are maintained by institutions including BIR, BOC, BTr and banks.

From authority to actual payment

DBM's cash authority governs permissible withdrawals or payments; an agency issues a payment instruction through the applicable mechanism; a servicing bank pays the recipient, with settlement and Treasury accounting handled under the relevant rules.

The route shown for an ordinary NGA MDS payment is not a universal rule for direct lender payments, remittances, GOCC accounts or local-government treasuries.
