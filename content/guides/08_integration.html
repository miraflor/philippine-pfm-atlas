UACS is a classification framework. IFMIS is an envisioned interconnected information environment. Neither, by itself, supplies the record-level linkage needed to trace a specific transaction.

Classes and identities

UACS can make budget, accounting and reporting amounts comparable by fund, organization, location, program/activity/project and object classification. One UACS line can cover many contracts, vouchers and payments. To follow an individual case, you need document numbers and evidence of the links between them.

Applications and exchanges

DBM, BTr, COA, BIR, BOC, procurement agencies, banks and others operate distinct applications. The atlas identifies existing applications and planned IFMIS integration centered on BTMS, but many operational API, payload, reconciliation and authoritative-record questions are still open.

Critical distinction: a documented information flow is not automatically an API, and a planned IFMIS architecture is not proof that a production interface is already deployed. Consult the systems and interface register.
