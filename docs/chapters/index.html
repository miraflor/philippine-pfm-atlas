Chapters

The chapters

The chapters follow the budget cycle in the illustration of DBM. Each of the four phases has a chapter that describes it as a whole, and each of its steps has a chapter of its own. Two chapters describe what all phases share: the information systems and UACS. The colours mark the lines to which a chapter belongs, as on the map.
