A contract that lasts more than one year: the Multi-Year Contractual Authority (MYCA)

Question: An appropriation is valid for one year, but a bridge takes three years to build. What allows an agency to sign one contract for the whole work, and where does the money of each year come from?

In: E1. Early procurement activities

What the diagram shows

  • Question: an appropriation is valid for one year, but a bridge takes three years to build. What allows an agency to sign one contract for the whole work, and where does the money of each year come from?
  • Idea: two different permissions are needed. The MYCA allows the agency to SIGN a contract for more than one year. The budget law of each year provides the MONEY of that year. The first does not replace the second.
  • How to read it: top = which authorization is needed, by source of money. Middle = from the MYCA to the contract. Bottom frame = what is repeated in every year of the contract.
  • Sources: the MYCA is defined as the authority to procure multi-year projects in the form and under the conditions set by DBM [IRR12009 s.5]. It is required before any procurement activity for locally funded multi-year contracts, DBM issues it, and its total project cost is the basis of the Approved Budget for the Contract. The cases by source of money: sections 9.1.1 to 9.1.4 [IRR12009 s.9]. Request documents, no change after issue, yearly amounts in the Tier 1 proposal, the note on the form about yearly obligations, and the rule that regular and recurring services no longer need a MYCA [CL2025-6]. DBM issues the MYCA under the cash budgeting order [EO91]. Renewal of contracts for regular and recurring services when funds are available [IRR12009 s.18].
  • From a press report of a DBM statement [GMA-MYCA]: the example of a bridge of PHP 10 billion programmed at 3, 4 and 3 billion in three years, and the statement that projects financed by foreign loans do not need a MYCA before the contract is signed.
  • Inference: the grey box at the bottom. No source that I read says what the agency does when the budget law of a year contains less than the amount planned for that year.

  S(["A project whose contract will last more than one year<br/>Example: a bridge of PHP 10 billion, planned as 3 + 4 + 3 billion in three years"])
  S --> Q{"Where does the<br/>money come from?"}

  Q -- "fully from the<br/>budget law (GAA)" --> R1["REQUEST TO DBM, BEFORE ANY PROCUREMENT ACTIVITY<br/>total project cost and schedule by year<br/>certification by the head of agency that procurement has not started<br/>commitment to keep the yearly amounts inside the budget ceilings<br/>funding strategy and medium-term expenditure program<br/>approvals that the project needs (for example the ICC)"]
  Q -- "partly own income of a state<br/>university or corporation" --> R2["Approval of the governing board,<br/>and a MYCA from DBM for the<br/>part paid from the GAA"]
  Q -- "fully own income" --> R3["Approval of the<br/>governing board only"]
  Q -- "local government" --> R4["Equivalent authorization<br/>of the sanggunian"]
  Q -- "foreign loan" --> R5["No MYCA before the contract<br/>(press report of a DBM statement)"]
  Q -- "regular and recurring<br/>service, for example rent" --> R6["No MYCA. The contract is renewed,<br/>if funds are available"]

  R1 --> M["MYCA ISSUED BY DBM<br/>states the total project cost and the amount for each year.<br/>It cannot be changed after it is issued"]:::auth
  R2 --> M
  M --> P["PROCUREMENT (see E1-b)<br/>the Approved Budget for the Contract is<br/>the total project cost stated in the MYCA"]
  P --> C["ONE CONTRACT FOR ALL YEARS IS SIGNED"]

  subgraph YEAR["REPEATED IN EVERY YEAR OF THE CONTRACT"]
    direction LR
    Y1["The amount for the year is put<br/>into the Tier 1 budget proposal<br/>of the agency (chapter P5)"]
    Y2["Congress appropriates the<br/>amount in the budget law<br/>of that year (chapter L0)"]
    Y3["The agency obligates only the part<br/>of that year. Obligations of a year<br/>may not exceed the appropriation<br/>of that year"]
    Y4["Delivery, acceptance and payment<br/>of that part (chapters E4 and E6)"]
    Y1 --> Y2 --> Y3 --> Y4
  end

  C --> Y1
  Y4 -. "next year" .-> Y1

  RULE["THE MYCA IS A PERMISSION TO SIGN. IT IS NOT AN APPROPRIATION.<br/>The money of each year still depends on the budget law of that year"]:::rule
  GAP["INFERENCE, NOT STATED IN A SOURCE: if the budget law of a year contains less than the planned amount,<br/>the agency cannot obligate the full part of that year, and the work or the payment schedule must change"]:::edge
