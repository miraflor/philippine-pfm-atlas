Budget legislation: from the President's budget to the General Appropriations Act (GAA)

Question: Which body acts on the budget bill at each stage, what may each body change, and what happens if no law is passed before the fiscal year starts?

In: L0. Legislation

What the diagram shows

  • Question: which body acts on the budget bill at each stage, what may each body change, and what happens if no law is passed before the fiscal year starts?
  • Sources: submission deadline [CONST VII.22]. Origin in the House, Senate may amend [CONST VI.24]. No increase above the President's recommendation, no rider, reenactment, augmentation only from savings [CONST VI.25]. Item veto [CONST VI.27(2)]. Three readings on separate days, printed copies of the final text three days before passage unless the President certifies the need for immediate enactment, no amendment at the last reading [CONST VI.26(2)]. Committee hearings, plenary, bicameral conference, ratification, transmission, signature, override by two thirds [INQ-GUIDE]. Senate hearings in parallel with the House [PRIMER]. Dates of the FY 2026 GAA and the four days of reenactment [NBC599].
  • From a press report: step 3. The House review of the FY 2027 budget opened on 17 August 2026 with a briefing by the DBCC [NEP2027-NEWS].
  • General knowledge, not opened in a source here: that the debate and the amendments belong to the second reading, the signing of the enrolled bill by the presiding officers, the veto message.

  participant PRES as President
  participant EXE as DBCC, DBM and agencies
  participant HCA as House Committee on Appropriations
  participant HP as House plenary
  participant SCF as Senate Committee on Finance
  participant SP as Senate plenary
  participant BCC as Bicameral Conference Committee

  PRES->>HP: President's budget: NEP, BESF, Budget Message,<br/>within 30 days from the opening of the regular session
  HP->>HCA: The NEP is filed as the General Appropriations Bill (GAB)<br/>and referred to the committee
  EXE->>HCA: Briefing on the economy and the fiscal program

  par House committee stage
    HCA->>EXE: Hearings, agency by agency: plans, past performance
    EXE-->>HCA: Answers and documents
  and Senate committee stage, at the same time
    SCF->>EXE: Hearings, agency by agency
    EXE-->>SCF: Answers and documents
  end

  HCA->>HP: Committee report on the GAB
  HP->>HP: Plenary debates and amendments.<br/>Approval on second and third reading<br/>#nbsp;
  HP->>SP: GAB as approved by the House
  SCF->>SP: Committee report with Senate amendments
  SP->>SP: Plenary debates and amendments.<br/>Approval on second and third reading<br/>#nbsp;

  Note over HCA,SP: LIMITS ON CONGRESS: it may not increase the appropriations recommended by the President.<br/>Every provision must relate to a specific appropriation in the bill (no rider).

  HP->>BCC: House conferees and House version
  SP->>BCC: Senate conferees and Senate version
  BCC->>BCC: Reconcile the two versions<br/>#nbsp;
  BCC-->>HP: Conference report
  BCC-->>SP: Conference report
  HP->>PRES: Both chambers ratify. The enrolled bill goes to the President
  PRES->>PRES: Review. May veto particular items.<br/>The items not vetoed become law<br/>#nbsp;
  PRES-->>EXE: Signed General Appropriations Act (GAA)

  opt Congress disagrees with a veto
    HP->>PRES: Override by two thirds of each chamber
  end

  Note over PRES,BCC: IF NO GAA IS PASSED BY THE END OF THE FISCAL YEAR: the GAA of the previous year is deemed re-enacted<br/>and stays in force until the new bill is passed. Example: the FY 2025 GAA was re-enacted from 1 to 4 January 2026.<br/>The FY 2026 GAA (Republic Act No. 12314) took effect on 5 January 2026.
