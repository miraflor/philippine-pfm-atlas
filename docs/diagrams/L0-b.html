  [*] --> NEP: President submits within 30 days from the opening of the regular session
  NEP --> HCOM: filed in the House and referred to the Committee on Appropriations
  HCOM --> HAPP: committee report, plenary debates, amendments, vote
  HAPP --> SAPP: Senate committee and plenary act on the House bill
  SAPP --> BICAM: the two versions differ
  BICAM --> RATIF: conferees agree, both chambers ratify
  RATIF --> ENR: sent to the President
  ENR --> GAA: President signs
  ENR --> VETO: President vetoes particular items
  VETO --> GAA: Congress overrides by two thirds of each chamber
  VETO --> [*]: the item is not law
  NEP --> REEN: the fiscal year starts and no GAA is in force
  REEN --> GAA: the new GAA takes effect (FY 2026 example, 5 January 2026)
  GAA --> [*]: execution starts (chapter E0)
