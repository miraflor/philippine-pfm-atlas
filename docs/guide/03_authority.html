Authority to commit is not cash

An introduction to the current procedural atlas; consult linked chapters and source notes before operational use.

An appropriation is not an obligation, an allotment is not a bank balance, and a Notice of Cash Allocation is not itself a transfer of cash.

Different permissions, different records

An appropriation provides legal spending authority under specified terms. The applicable allotment mechanism permits the agency to incur obligations. A valid obligation records a commitment. Cash authority subsequently limits permitted disbursement under the relevant banking arrangement.

The atlas distinguishes GAA-as-allotment-order from SARO or GARO cases and other special arrangements. These vary by fiscal year and classification: consult Chapter 05 rather than applying one release instrument universally.

Control check: Neither a release document nor a UACS code proves that a particular supplier received money. The payment and settlement records are separate evidence.
