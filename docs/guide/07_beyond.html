LGUs, GOCCs, payroll and future risks

An introduction to the current procedural atlas; consult linked chapters and source notes before operational use.

The national-government contract-payment pattern is only one route through PFM. Other institutional and financial arrangements have their own legal cycles and records.

Follow the exceptions

Employee compensation relies on positions, appointments, personnel service and payroll instead of bidding and delivery. Local governments enact and execute their own budgets, while interacting with national bodies through tax transfers, reporting and review. GOCCs use corporate budgeting and support arrangements. Special fund clusters, disaster-response mechanisms, interagency transfers and multi-year commitments create further paths.

Study these as separate control patterns; do not describe them as variants that simply skip ordinary procurement boxes.
