Research model RM-2

Reconciliation across budget, accounting, bank and physical evidence

What it is for. A proposed way to sort the differences that a reconciliation finds: differences of timing, of classification, of a missing link between records, and irregularities.

Why it is not in a chapter. It is a proposed procedure. The atlas describes the differences themselves in chapter A0, section 4, and the reconciliations that the Manual requires in chapter A4.
