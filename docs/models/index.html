What these models add

They distinguish transaction identities from UACS codes; permissions from actual financial balances; source-of-record ownership from data transport; concurrent accounting from a linear payment trail; and current deployment evidence from planned IFMIS integration. These are research claims that require reconciliation with applicable legal texts and agency system specifications.
