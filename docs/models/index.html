Research models

Research models and registers

33 research models, 3 research references and 6 registers of typed events, records, rules, authorities and interfaces.

Not part of the chapters. A research model is a proposal, a method or a general model. It is not a description of Philippine PFM from a source, so it is kept apart from the chapters. Each model page says what the model is for, and why it is not in a chapter.

The research models

RM-1 · Shared reference model of a financial eventNames the parts that one financial event connects: the authority, the rule, the input and output records, the classification, the system, the balance that changes and the delivered output. It gives the terms that the research registers use.RM-2 · Reconciliation across budget, accounting, bank and physical evidenceA proposed way to sort the differences that a reconciliation finds: differences of timing, of classification, of a missing link between records, and irregularities.RM-3 · Revenue administration beyond collecting taxThe steps of revenue administration before and after a collection: registration, declaration or assessment, arrears and enforcement, matching of records, refunds and adjustments.RM-4 · Entity and funding channel variantsThe kinds of public body whose money follows other rules than a national agency: local governments, government corporations, government financial institutions, the Bangsamoro region, and special funds.RM-5 · IFMIS implementation is not equivalent to an interfaceA method to grade a statement that two information systems exchange data: from a planned interface to an interface that is shown to work, with the evidence that each grade needs.RM-6 · IFMIS interface failure and duplicate entry patternThe pattern of an exchange between two systems that fails: a record that is missing, duplicated, old, different or in the wrong period, and the owner who must correct it.RM-7 · UACS code revisions and crosswalk controlsA proposed control for comparing data that use two versions of the UACS codes: a versioned mapping, and the checks for codes that are split, merged or removed.RM-8 · Typed financial records with stable IDs and versioned classificationA proposed data schema for typed financial records with stable identifiers and versioned classification. It is the schema behind the research registers.
