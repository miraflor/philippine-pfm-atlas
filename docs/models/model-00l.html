' QUESTION THIS DIAGRAM ANSWERS: Who works concurrently across budget execution and accounting?
' SOURCES: Reference activity model [GAM] [NBC599]
@startuml
title 00l Parallel PFM workstreams by authority
skinparam shadowing false
|#EEF4FB|DBM|
start
:Release applicable allotments under FY rules;
fork
  |#EDF7ED|Agency procurement and programs|
  :Plan / procure / award under legal conditions;
  :Document contract, obligation and receipt of goods/services;
fork again
  |#FFF8DC|DBM, BTr and banks|
  :Receive revenue and financing information;
  :Program cash and arrange payment authority;
fork again
  |#F2F2F2|Agency budget and accounting units|
  :Record opening authorities and transactions as they arise;
  :Reconcile registers, journals, subledgers and third-party statements;
end fork
|#EDF7ED|Agency authorized payment officials|
:Validate transaction-specific claim and cash/payment controls;
|#FFF8DC|Bank and Treasury|
:Settle and reconcile payment;
fork
  |#F2F2F2|Agency accounting and budget units|
  :Post settlement / adjustments / report and reconcile;
fork again
  |#FDF1E3|COA and assurance functions|
  :Examine transactions, balances, controls, performance;
  :Issue findings as applicable;
end fork
|#EEF4FB|Agency management and budget authorities|
:Correct records and systems; apply lessons to subsequent budgets;
stop
