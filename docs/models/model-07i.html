  %% SOURCES: [RA12009] [IRR12009] [NBC599] [GAM] - process abstraction
  %% SCOPE: Reference view; not a verified deployed system interface.
  %% READING: Arrows show possible evidence, authority or event dependencies, not a universal time order or verified API.
flowchart TB
  NEED["Approved need / plan / procurement method"] --> PROC["Procurement and contract decision"]
  AL["Appropriation and valid allotment / funding"] --> AWARD{"Contract award and obligation conditions met?"}
  PROC --> AWARD
  AWARD -->|No| WAIT["Do not award / commit until legal conditions met"]
  AWARD -->|Yes| OB["Award, contract, obligation recorded"]
  OB --> BILL{"Nature of claim?"}
  BILL -->|Delivered goods / services| ACC["Inspect / accept and approve payable"]
  BILL -->|Progress billing| PROG["Certify measured accomplishment and contract terms"]
  BILL -->|Lawful advance| ADV["Check authorization, security and subsequent liquidation requirements"]
  ACC --> DV["Validate disbursement claim and voucher"]
  PROG --> DV
  ADV --> DV
  DV --> CASH["Confirm applicable cash/payment authority and bank checks"]
  CASH --> PAY["Pay and settle"] --> RECON["Record, reconcile, audit evidence retained"]
