Reference J — Control gates, exception patterns, and time

1. Replace one universal payment gate with payment families

Family Typical initiating event Critical evidence (illustrative, not exhaustive) Why a delivered-goods rule can fail
Standard contractor invoice Delivery / work achievement Contract, obligation, acceptance/progress evidence, approved claim, valid authority Correct main pattern; but progress bills and advances have conditional documentation
Lawful advance or mobilization Contractual advance authorized Contract conditions, guarantees/security if required, liquidation Payment may precede final acceptance
Personnel Service / entitlement in payroll period Lawful appointment, payroll certifications, deductions, funding No bidding or supplier acceptance
Cash benefit / assistance Beneficiary entitlement Program rules, eligibility, approved distribution / controls Payment is not to supplier for accepted goods
Interagency / LGU / GOCC transfer Lawful transfer decision Appropriation/automatic share, transfer conditions, reporting Receiving unit has its own accounting process
Debt service Interest/principal becomes due Debt instrument, payment schedule and statutory authority No procurement / inspection gate
Tax refund Valid refundable tax / final determination Claim, original collection/assessment, offset, refund authority Corrects revenue and produces outgoing cash

No row is a complete legal checklist. FY validity, delegation, amount thresholds, case-specific rules, and controls must be checked against the primary issuances. RA 12009 official IRR advisory cautions against citing withdrawn 2026 IRR text.

2. The temporal model

A transaction has at least four dates: authority_effective, economic_event, record_posting, and cash_settlement. Another clock describes the FY and appropriation validity. The clocks need not match. FY 2026 agency action might pay an older payable under a lawful special validity rule; audit may occur later, while estimates for FY 2027 are already being prepared.

3. Accounting is event-driven

Record items when recognition criteria are met, not only after cash moves. Accrual accounting may recognize a payable at delivery, a receivable prior to collection or asset changes independent of cash settlement. Bank settlement supplies separate evidence. Budgetary and financial accounting should be reconciled, not treated as identical.

4. Audit is not a terminal payment approval

Agency management controls operate during procurement, obligation, payment, accounting and reporting. Internal audit is separate where established. COA's independent audit may examine transactions and balances over time; specific notices, appeals and outcomes require the governing COA rules and procedural facts. Avoid the inference that every payment must wait for COA's audit.
