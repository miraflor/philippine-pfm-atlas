Part of the Philippine PFM Atlas. Facts as of 8 October 2026. The codes in the column "Basis" are explained in Reference F. "GK" means general knowledge that I did not open in a source during this work.

This file lists every body that appears in the diagrams. For each body it gives the role in public financial management (PFM), the systems it owns, and the chapters where it acts. Diagram 00b shows the same bodies in groups. Diagram 00g shows what passes between them.

The roles of the units inside one agency (section 6) are taken from the Government Accounting Manual and its forms, from the Government Auditing Code and from the Implementing Rules of the procurement law. I read these texts as files.

1. Congress

Authority Role in PFM Chapters Basis
House of Representatives Appropriation, revenue and tariff bills must start in the House. It receives the President's budget, holds hearings through its Committee on Appropriations, and passes the General Appropriations Bill. Tax bills pass through its Committee on Ways and Means 04, 10 CONST VI.24. Names of committees: INQ-GUIDE, PRIMER
Senate May propose or concur with amendments to the House bill. Its Committee on Finance holds hearings at the same time as the House. It joins the bicameral conference 04 CONST VI.24, INQ-GUIDE, PRIMER
Bicameral Conference Committee Members of both chambers reconcile the two versions of the bill. Both chambers then ratify the result 04 INQ-GUIDE
Congress as a whole May not increase the appropriations recommended by the President. May override a veto by two thirds. Receives the annual report of COA. Receives notices and reports required by the budget law 04, 08, 09 CONST VI.25(1), IX-D.4, INQ-GUIDE, BSGC2026
Oversight committees of both chambers Review spending and audit findings. The 2025 PEFA assessment found no formal scrutiny by the legislature of the audit reports on the annual financial reports 09 Names and work of the committees: GK. Finding: PEFA2025

2. President and collegial bodies of the Executive

Authority Role in PFM Chapters Basis
President Submits the budget to Congress within 30 days from the opening of the regular session. Signs the General Appropriations Act and may veto particular items. May contract or guarantee foreign loans with the prior concurrence of the Monetary Board. May augment items of the Executive from savings, as authorized by law 03, 04, 05, 11 CONST VII.22, VI.27(2), VII.20, VI.25(5)
Office of the President (OP) Approves some releases that the release guidelines reserve for it. Issues the Special Authority to negotiate and sign loans. Is represented in the DBCC and other committees 02, 05, 11 NBC599, MC51, DBCC-PAGE, AO37, EO108
Economy and Development Council (ED Council) Successor of the NEDA Board, headed by the President. Approves development plans and major projects. Its committees include the DBCC, the ICC, the Infrastructure Committee, the Social Development Committee, the Economic Development Committee and the Tariff and Related Matters Committee 01, 02 AO37. Functions: GK
Development Budget Coordination Committee (DBCC) Recommends the level of the annual expenditure program and the ceilings, the split between current operating expenditures and capital outlays, the revenue estimates, tax measures and the level of borrowing. Issues the Fiscal Risks Statement. Sets the target amount of the PPP Risk Management Fund. Chair: DBM Secretary. Co-chairperson: Secretary of Finance. Members: DEPDev and the Office of the President. BSP is a resource institution. An Executive Technical Board prepares its work 02, 03, 11, 19 DBCC-PAGE, AO37, FRS2023, RA11966 s.27
Investment Coordination Committee (ICC) Appraises large projects before they are funded: projects of PHP 5 billion or more funded by the national budget, projects under public-private partnership, and every project funded by a foreign loan. Under the Partnership Code it recommends national partnership projects of PHP 15 billion or more to the Council, and it decides on smaller ones that need money of the budget law 01, 11, 19 ICC-NEWS, AO37, RA11966 s.7
Infrastructure Committee (InfraCom) Works on the Three-Year Rolling Infrastructure Program 01 NBM156, AO37
Regional Development Councils (RDCs) One per region. Endorse regional priority programs and projects and give inputs on the regional budget proposals of agencies 01, 03 NBM156
PFM Committee DBM, DOF, COA and BTr. Steers PFM reform and the Integrated Financial Management Information System. Resource institutions: DICT and DEPDev. Principals: DBM Secretary, Secretary of Finance, COA Chairperson 16 EO29, ROADMAP2024
Government Procurement Policy Board (GPPB) and its Technical Support Office Sets procurement rules and issues the Implementing Rules of the procurement law. Chairperson: DBM Secretary. Alternate chairperson: the head of the planning department (the Rules still use the name NEDA). Members: the Secretaries of Public Works and Highways, National Defense, Education, the Interior and Local Government, and Transportation, a representative of PS-DBM, and a representative of the private sector. COA may be invited as resource person. The Technical Support Office is an agency attached to DBM. The GPPB may add a type of negotiated procurement under four conditions, and it added one in January 2026. It issues guidelines that it declares appendices or annexes of the Rules. Its Technical Support Office receives the procurement plans and the monitoring reports of all procuring entities, since 2026 through an online form 06 RA12009 s.91, IRR12009 s.91.2, s.91.3, s.35, GPPB-ADV, GPPB-LIST, GPPB2026-01, GPPB-TSO2026-2
Permanent Committee for special, fiduciary and trust funds The Secretary of Finance as chair, the DBM Secretary and the COA Chairperson. Makes the rules under which receipts are recorded as income of a fund other than the General Fund. Monitors these funds and may recommend to the President that an amount returns to the General Fund. I did not check how this committee works today 10, 15 PD1445 ss.65-66, EO292-B6 s.45, GAM-V1 Ch.5 s.4
PPP Governing Board "the overall policy-making body for all PPP-related matters". Chair: head of the planning department. Vice-chair: Secretary of Finance. DBM is a member 19 RA11966 s.25
COMPASS Council Created by Executive Order No. 126 of 2026 for the public portal of budget and spending data. Chair: DBM. Co-chair: DICT. Members: DOF and DILG. Approves policies and data standards, monitors compliance, meets at least once in each quarter 16, 18 EO126 (read in press releases, not in the text of the order)
Technical working group under Executive Order No. 61 Reviews the performance management and incentive systems of the government, and issues the yearly rules for the Performance-Based Bonus during the review. Chair: DBM Secretary. Co-chair: Executive Secretary. Members: the Secretary of Finance, the head of the planning department, the head of the Anti-Red Tape Authority 18 EO61, PBB2024
National disaster council (National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council), with the Office of Civil Defense Recommends to the President the use of the national disaster fund 15, 19 GAA2026-NDRRMF

3. Central finance, budget and planning agencies

Authority Role in PFM Systems it owns Chapters Basis
Department of Budget and Management (DBM) Issues the Budget Call, evaluates proposals, prepares the President's budget. Releases allotments and cash authority. By the Administrative Code its Secretary approves each request for allotment, may change an allotment that was issued, and decides the categories in which spending is recorded. Issues the Multi-Year Contractual Authority. Receives execution plans and accountability reports. Conducts the yearly Agency Performance Review. Reviews corporate operating budgets and the budgets of provinces, highly urbanized cities and independent component cities. Keeps the list of authorized positions. Leads the BTMS project. Operates the COMPASS portal and chairs its council OSBPS, BPMS, GMIS, e-Budget (DBM), ADRS, URS, BTMS, DIME, COMPASS portal 03, 05, 06, 08, 12, 14, 15, 16, 18 NBM156, NBC599, EO292-B6 s.33, s.53, CL2025-6, JC2019-1, PBB2024, COB, LGC s.326, GMIS, CL2025-8, EO126
DBM regional offices Work with the regional offices of agencies and with local governments 05, 14 GK
Procurement Service (PS-DBM) Buys common-use supplies and equipment centrally for all agencies, which must buy these items from it when it has them. Manages PhilGEPS, with a Virtual Store and an Electronic Marketplace for these items. In the Electronic Marketplace it validates the orders and pays the merchant after the entity accepts the goods. Since October 2025 it publishes the registry of the beneficial owners of bidders through PhilGEPS, on an interim authority of the GPPB PhilGEPS 06 RA12009 s.20, s.40, IRR12009 s.20.1.1, s.40.1, PS-FAQ, GPPB2025-10, GPPB2025-11
Department of Finance (DOF) Revenue policy and revenue forecasts. Borrowing and debt policy. Negotiates and signs foreign loans. Co-chairs the DBCC. Supervises BIR, BOC, BTr and BLGF. Clears the undertakings of the national government in partnership contracts. Prepares the table of the consolidated public sector position. Its Secretary certifies the schedules of market values of local governments and chairs the steering committee of the National Single Window. Member of the COMPASS Council TradeNet (with DICT) 02, 08, 10, 11, 14, 19 AO37, MC51, ODA, RA11966 s.8, BESF2026-A3, RA12001 s.16, TRADENET, EO126. Supervision of bureaus: GK
Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) Assesses and collects internal revenue taxes. The money is paid through authorized agent banks, electronic gateways and revenue collection officers. Decides claims for refund, which are paid from an amount in its budget eFPS, eBIRForms, ePay gateways 10 BIR-PAY, GAA2025-BIR, TRIB-REFUND
Bureau of Customs (BOC) Assesses and collects duties and taxes on imports. Banks send abstracts of collection to BOC through the Payment Application Secure system E2M customs system. A Customs Processing System is planned 10 PAS, BOC-MOD
Bureau of the Treasury (BTr) Manages the cash of the national government and the Treasury Single Account. Issues, services and redeems government securities. Controls and services public debt. Keeps the books of cash transactions of the national government. Is the principal custodian of its financial assets. Bonds accountable officers. Releases shares to local governments and budgetary support to corporations. Keeps the National Asset Registry. National money leaves the Treasury only upon a warrant or another instrument of withdrawal that the Secretary of Finance approves on the recommendation of the Treasurer eTRAMS, NGCDS, NRoSS, NARS 05, 10, 11, 13, 14, 15 EO449, BTR-SITE, BTR-SYS, NBC599, PD1445 s.84, GAM-V1 Ch.2 s.8
Bureau of Local Government Finance (BLGF) Receives the statements of receipts and expenditures of local governments and maintains the system for them. Reviews the schedules of market values. Certifies the borrowing capacity of a local government before a loan eSRE. A database of real property transactions is required by the law of 2024 14, 19 eSRE, RA12001, DOF-LGULOAN
Department of Economy, Planning, and Development (DEPDev) Former NEDA. Coordinates the development plan and the Public Investment Program. Is secretariat of the ED Council and its committees. Issues the National Evaluation Policy Framework with DBM, and chairs its steering committee, with DBM as co-chair. Its head chairs the PPP Governing Board PIPOL 01, 02, 18, 19 PIP-MEMO, NBM156, SFA11, NEPF, RA11966 s.25. Secretariat role: GK
Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) Receives copies of local development plans. Supervises local governments in general for the President. Member of the COMPASS Council 14, 16 LGC s.287, EO126. Supervision: GK
Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) Supports the IFMIS as resource institution. Leads the eGovPay platform with the working group on digital payments. Co-chairs the COMPASS Council and sets the standards of the portal for interoperability, cybersecurity and data management. Operates the national public key infrastructure. Built TradeNet eGovPay (planned), PNPKI 16 EO29, SFA6, EO126, PNPKI, TRADENET
PPP Center Assists agencies in partnerships with private firms, reviews contracts, monitors projects and keeps the project documents. Manages the Project Development and Monitoring Facility and the PPP Risk Management Fund. Reports each year to Congress 19 RA11966 ss.16, 24, 26, 27
Anti-Red Tape Authority Validates the criterion "process results" of the Performance-Based Bonus. Member of the working group under Executive Order No. 61 18 PBB2024, EO61
Department of Justice (DOJ) Gives the legal opinion on the authority of the borrower in a foreign loan 11 ODA
Governance Commission for GOCCs (GCG) Oversees the government corporations that the law places under it 15 COB (coverage only). Its work: GK

4. Independent bodies

Authority Role in PFM Systems it owns Chapters Basis
Commission on Audit (COA) Examines, audits and settles all accounts of the government. Has exclusive authority to define the scope of its audit and to issue accounting and auditing rules. Keeps the general accounts of the government, prescribes the chart of accounts, and issued the Government Accounting Manual. Reports each year to the President and Congress. Audits after payment since 2011. Its auditors issue notices of suspension, disallowance and charge. Audits partnership projects, and adopts their accounting and auditing framework together with the PPP Center eNGAS, eBudget (COA), AFR System 07, 08, 09, 19 CONST IX-D.2, IX-D.4, PD1445 s.26, s.33, s.113, COA2015-007, COA2011-002, COA2009-006, COA-GAS, RA11966 s.31
COA Government Accountancy Sector Receives the financial statements and trial balances of agencies and prepares the Annual Financial Report. Keeps budget records of the whole approved budget and of its execution, with which the registries of each agency are reconciled AFR System 08 COA-GAS, COA2015-004, GAM-V1 Ch.2 s.8, Ch.19 s.5
Civil Service Commission (CSC) Rules on appointments and personnel 12 GK
Office of the Ombudsman and the Sandiganbayan Investigate and try public officials 09 GK
Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Holds the Treasury Single Account. Operates the settlement system through which payments between banks and the Treasury are settled. Its Monetary Board must concur before the President contracts a foreign loan, and gives an opinion on each loan of a local government before the loan is released. Is resource institution of the DBCC Settlement system 02, 07, 11, 14 ROADMAP2024, NRoSS, CONST VII.20, DBCC-PAGE, DOF-LGULOAN

5. Banks and payment bodies

Authority Role in PFM Chapters Basis
Government servicing banks: Land Bank of the Philippines (LBP), Development Bank of the Philippines (DBP), Philippine Veterans Bank (PVB) Hold the sub-accounts of agencies under the Modified Disbursement System (MDS). Record the NCA as a limit. Pay the payees named in agency payment orders. Report the payments to BTr and are replenished. Send to each agency the monthly bank statement, with which the agency reconciles its books 05, 07, 08, 11 NBC599, CL2013-16A, eMDS, GAM-V1 Ch.6 s.2, Ch.21 s.5
Authorized agent banks Receive tax and customs payments and remit them to the Treasury 10 BIR-PAY, PAS, PEFA2025
Authorized government depository banks Receive the collections of agencies. Upload collection reports to BTr. Hold the accounts of revolving funds and retained income 10, 11 EO449, BTR-SYS, GAA2026-GP
Philippine Clearing House Corporation (PCHC) Operates Payment Application Secure for the banks, by which abstracts of customs collections reach BOC 10 PAS

6. Bodies that spend public money

Authority Role in PFM Chapters Basis
Departments and agencies of the national government (NGAs), with state universities and colleges Prepare budget proposals, receive allotments, procure, obligate, verify, pay, record and report. Each keeps a complete set of accounting books by fund cluster, and budget registries. An operating unit is an entity that receives release documents directly from DBM all NBM156, NBC599, GAM-V1 Ch.2 s.8
Head of agency (also head of the procuring entity) Is "immediately and primarily responsible for all government funds and property" of the agency, and must install and monitor a system of internal control. Approves the procurement plan and decides on the award of each contract. Approves each payment: Box D of the voucher and Box I of the payment order, and countersigns the instruction to the bank. Approves changes inside an allotment class. Answers audit findings 05, 06, 07, 09 PD1445 s.102, s.124, s.91. RA12009 s.7, s.66, IRR12009 s.7.7.5, s.66.2. GAM-V2 App.32. GAM-V1 Ch.6 s.51. CL2013-16A, NBC599
Budget unit of an agency Keeps the registries of appropriations, allotments, obligations and disbursements, and the registries of revenue. These registries have no double entry. Assigns the number of each obligation request and certifies in its Box B that allotment is available and obligated. Prepares the budget reports and the statement that compares budget and actual amounts 07, 08 GAM-V1 Ch.2 s.36, Ch.3 ss.5-15, ss.25-30. GAM-V2 App.8, App.9A, App.11. JC2019-1
Accounting unit of an agency Certifies that funds are available before a contract is signed. Keeps the registry that compares allotments with cash authority. Examines each claim, assigns the voucher number, and certifies in Box C of the voucher that cash is available and that the documents are complete. Makes the journal entries, certifies Box I of the payment order, keeps the ledgers and the ledger cards for supplies and property, reconciles the books with the bank statement each month, and prepares the trial balances and the financial statements. No member of the accounting unit may be designated to draw checks, and the chief accountant cannot be a regular member of the Bids and Awards Committee 07, 08, 13 PD1445 s.86, s.92. EO292-B6 s.40. GAM-V1 Ch.2 s.36, Ch.6 s.3, s.12, s.51, Ch.19, Ch.21 s.4. GAM-V2 App.30, App.32, App.36. IRR12009 s.41.2.5. CL2013-16A
Cash unit of an agency, with its cashier, disbursing officers and collecting officers Prepares checks, and signs the instruction to the bank in Box II of the payment order. Records each check and each payment order with the new balance of the NCA. Delivers the payment order to the servicing bank and obtains the validated copy. Prepares each day the report of checks issued and the report of payment orders issued. Collecting officers issue an official receipt for every payment, report the collections each day and deposit them. These officers are accountable for the money and are bonded 07, 10 GAM-V1 Ch.6 s.10, s.12, ss.51-56, Ch.5 ss.39-40. GAM-V2 App.13, App.34, App.35, App.39. PD1445 ss.68-69, s.101. CL2013-16A
Bids and Awards Committee (BAC), with its Secretariat and technical working group Conducts procurement: publishes, receives and opens bids, evaluates, post-qualifies, recommends award. It has five or seven members, whom the head of the procuring entity designates. The head cannot be a member. The Secretariat is the procurement unit, and it consolidates the plans of the end-user units into the Annual Procurement Plan 06 RA12009 ss.41-44, IRR12009 s.41.2, s.42.1, s.44
End-user unit, also called the requesting office States the need: prepares its procurement plan for each project, the specifications and the purchase request. Its head certifies in Box A of the obligation request, and its supervisor in Box A of the voucher, that the expense is necessary and lawful and was made under his or her direct supervision 06, 07 RA12009, IRR12009 s.7.7.1. GAM-V1 Ch.3 s.11, Ch.8 s.13. GAM-V2 App.11, App.32, App.60
Inspection officer or inspection committee, and supply or property unit The inspection and acceptance report has two different signers. The inspection officer or committee certifies that the quantity and the specifications are in order. The supply or property custodian accepts the delivery. The property unit keeps the stock cards and the property cards, issues property to users against a receipt, and participates in the physical count 07, 13 GAM-V1 Ch.8 s.15, Ch.10 s.38, s.42. GAM-V2 App.58, App.62, App.69, App.71, App.73
Personnel unit Keeps the list of positions in GMIS and prepares the payroll. On the payroll an authorized official certifies that the services were rendered 12 GMIS. Certificate: GAM-V2 App.33. Who prepares the payroll: GK
Internal audit unit Reviews internal controls for the head of agency 09 ROADMAP2024 (standards named). Role: GK
Government-owned or controlled corporations (GOCCs) Spend under a corporate operating budget approved by the governing board and reviewed by DBM. Some receive subsidy or equity from the national budget. Remit dividends 15 COB, BSGC2026
Local government units (LGUs): provinces, cities, municipalities, barangays Have their own budgets, enacted by the sanggunian (local legislature) and executed by the local chief executive, the treasurer, the accountant and the budget officer. Receive the National Tax Allotment 14 LGC, CONST X.5, X.6
Local finance committee Local planning and development officer, local budget officer and local treasurer. Estimates income and recommends spending ceilings 14 LGC s.316
Provincial, city or municipal assessor Prepares the Schedule of Market Values, from which the real property tax is computed 14 RA12001 s.15
Barangay: punong barangay, sangguniang barangay, barangay treasurer The punong barangay prepares the budget with the barangay treasurer. The sangguniang barangay enacts it. The sanggunian of the city or municipality reviews it. The barangay treasurer has custody of the funds 14 BB4
Department of Social Welfare and Development, as one example of an agency that pays cash aid Selects the households of the conditional cash transfer program and pays the grants through authorized government depository banks. Holds one of the two largest Quick Response Funds for disasters 15 RA11310 ss.5, 9, GAA2026-NDRRMF

7. Parties outside government

Party Role in PFM Chapters Basis
Taxpayers and importers File returns and declarations, pay taxes and duties through banks 10 BIR-PAY, PAS
Suppliers, contractors and consultants Register in PhilGEPS, bid, post securities, perform contracts, receive payment in their bank accounts 06, 07 RA12009, IRR12009 s.20.2.9, s.52.1, s.68, CL2013-16A
Observers in procurement At least two per proceeding, from the private sector and from civil society, in addition to the representative of COA. They have no vote. They report to the head of the procuring entity, to PhilGEPS and to COA 06 RA12009 s.43, IRR12009 s.43.1, s.43.4
Dealers in government securities and other lenders Bid in the auctions of BTr, or lend under loan agreements 11 NRoSS, MC51
Private partner of a partnership Finances, builds or operates a public facility under a partnership contract, and is paid by the government or by the users 19 RA11966
Persons who receive cash aid Are named in the list of beneficiaries of a program and are paid through a bank or a remittance institution 15 RA11310 s.9
Civil society organizations and citizens Are consulted in budget preparation. Join selected audits of COA (citizen participatory audit). Can check release documents on the DBM verification page, and can read budget and spending data in the COMPASS portal 03, 09, 16 NBM156, ROADMAP2024, DBM-BLOCKCHAIN, COMPASS-LAUNCH
