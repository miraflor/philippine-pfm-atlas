1. Three roles around each system

For each system it is useful to separate three roles, because they are often held by three different institutions.

  • The owner builds and controls the system.
  • The user is the office that enters the data.
  • The recipient is the office that receives or reads the data.

Example: COA owns eNGAS, the accounting unit of each agency is the user, and COA auditors are among the recipients. A table that names only the owner hides who is responsible for the correctness of the data, and that is the user.

Diagram 00c shows the systems by owner. Diagrams 16b and 16g show what passes between them, and diagram 08a shows what passes between the units of one agency. Section 4 of this file lists every one of these flows with its kind, its carrier, its stage of use, the control that compares both sides, and the evidence. Diagram 16a shows the systems by PFM function.

4. Register of interfaces: every flow between two systems or bodies, with the evidence for it

A diagram of systems is quick to draw and difficult to verify, because an arrow between two boxes can mean very different things: a program that sends data to another program, a person who types the same data again, a paper that is carried, a movement of money, or a plan. One word for the state of an arrow cannot hold all of this. The register therefore answers five separate questions for each flow of diagrams 16b, 16g and 08a.

Column Question Values
Kind What passes? Data: figures or records. Document: a signed paper, or a copy or file of it. Authority: a document that gives a right or sets a limit, for example an allotment or a Notice of Cash Allocation. Money: money moves between two accounts or persons
Carrier How does it pass? System to system: one program sends to another, and no person copies the content. Entry by the sender: a person of the sending body enters or uploads the content in a system of the receiving body. Download by the receiver: a person of the receiving body takes a file from a system of the sending body. Entry in own system: staff enter the content in a record or system of their own body. Electronic, kind not stated: a source describes an electronic channel and does not say which of the kinds above it is. Paper: signed paper copies are sent or carried. Bank transfer and Cash or check: for money. Public website: published for any person. Not stated: a source says that the content passes, and not how
Stage of use Is it used today? In use. In use for part: only some agencies or banks use it. Described or required, use not verified: a rule or the owner describes it, and no source confirms the use. Planned. Not stated
Check of both sides Which control compares what the sender recorded with what the receiver recorded? Free text. "None stated" means that I read no such control
Basis Where did I read it? A source code of Reference F. "GK" means that the flow itself is from general knowledge. For such a row the carrier and the stage are "Not stated"

Each row has a number that stays fixed, so that other files can cite it. A row that is added receives the next free number and stands in the table of its side. A row that is removed leaves its number unused.

One rule of reading: "Not stated" never means "absent". It means that the sources that I read do not say. A flow with the carrier "Not stated" may be a file, a paper, a second entry by a person, or an interface between two programs.

A case where the register is useful: a model assumes that an obligation recorded by an agency is known to DBM immediately. Row 15 shows that the obligation is entered by agency staff in a registry of the agency, and row 12 shows that DBM receives the figures through reports, which are quarterly for obligations. The assumption is wrong for every agency that is not on BTMS.

A second case: a model assumes that the bank and the Treasury learn of a payment from a system. Rows 32 to 34 and row 19 show that the Manual of 2015 prescribes paper copies between the agency, the bank and the Treasury, and row 17 shows an electronic facility that only the agencies with accounts at one bank can use.

A case where the register is not enough: the stage "In use" says that a source describes the flow as existing. It does not say that the flow works without error or delay, and it does not say what the receiving side does with the content. The last column names the control that could show this, where I read one.

The register has 69 rows. 10 of them are movements of money. Of the other 59 rows:

  • 5 have the carrier "System to system". The number of these with the stage "In use" for all users is 1.

  • 10 pass on paper.
  • 5 are downloads or public websites.
  • 4 have an electronic channel whose kind the sources do not state.

These numbers describe what the sources say. They are not a measurement of the systems.

5. Status of BTMS in October 2026

Fact Basis
BTMS was first adopted for budget execution by DBM Circular Letter No. 2019-4, and later revived CL2025-8
The present system was launched in May 2025. It processed about PHP 4.25 billion from its first transactions on 17 December 2025 to the end of that year INQ-BTMS
DBM Circular Letter No. 2025-8 of 28 August 2025 makes BTMS the primary system for expenditure transactions of all national agencies with appropriations in the GAA, in a phased rollout. DBM issues the rollout schedule CL2025-8
DBM and BTr use the system. Five more pilot agencies prepare in 2026. Plan: 110 agencies in 2027 and 198 in 2028, total 315. The action plan of the Roadmap counts 2, then 115, then 198 INQ-BTMS, SFA6
A third source gives other dates and numbers. In a speech of 22 January 2026 the Secretary of Budget and Management said that BTMS was launched in 2024, was piloted with seven agencies in 2025, and would reach 155 national agencies in 2026. The atlas states the three sources and does not decide between them DBM-SPEECH26
Target share of the budget executed through BTMS: 50 percent, then 80 percent SFA6
A third-party audit and certification of BTMS is planned for 2026 and 2027 SFA6
Reports are submitted through URS or other electronic means until agencies migrate to BTMS GAA2026-GP
Governance: PFM Committee, a Central Project Management Group, and the DBM project management office. Each agency forms a project implementation team, prepares a security access matrix and migrates its data CL2025-8
Interfaces (APIs) are foreseen with procurement, document management, electronic funds transfer and financial systems. Which interfaces are already in use: not verified CL2025-8

6. What is not known from the sources I read

  • The functions of the systems PMIS, BTr NGAS and Online Cash Planning Reporting System, which the Roadmap lists by name only.
  • The channel by which an NCA reaches the servicing bank.
  • How data pass today from PIPOL to OSBPS.
  • How many agencies send payment orders to the bank on paper, and how many use an electronic facility of a bank.
  • The dates and numbers of the rollout of BTMS, on which three sources disagree (section 5).
  • Which features of the modernized PhilGEPS are in use in October 2026. A resolution of September 2025 gives the state at that time (section 2).
  • How a procuring entity deposits money in the eWallet of the Electronic Marketplace, and how its books show the balance.
  • The internal data model of BTMS.
  • The system that supplies the figures of the COMPASS portal, the frequency of its updates, and the text of Executive Order No. 126.
  • The present state of TradeNet, of the Customs Processing System and of the Real Property Information System.
  • Whether certificates of the PNPKI are used to sign PFM documents.
  • The network and record format of the DBM blockchain application. Press reports that quote the vendor name a public network and a token for each document. The DBM page does not state them.
