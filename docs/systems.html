Systems and institutional responsibilities

Authorities, UACS and IFMIS

An authority grants permission. A document records an event. UACS classifies financial information. An information system may record or exchange that information. These are different relationships.

Evidence boundary: The atlas does not establish that every depicted system connection is a functioning automated interface. Deployment, exchange mechanism and reconciliation must be verified individually. Target IFMIS architecture is not production evidence.
