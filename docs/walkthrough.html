Guided walkthrough

Understand the system in eight lessons

A guided route from public priorities and budget authority to procurement, actual cash settlement, concurrent accounting and the planned IFMIS environment.

The lessons come from the walkthrough edition; each now points into the reference edition’s stable diagrams and fully source-linked chapters. This tour is a teaching entry point, not a replacement for procedure-specific legal controls.
