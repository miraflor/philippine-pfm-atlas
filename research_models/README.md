# Research models

This folder holds analytical material that is not part of the chapters of the atlas. The website shows it under
"Research models".

| Folder or file | Content |
|---|---|
| `diagrams/` | Eight research models, RM-1 to RM-8, in Mermaid or PlantUML |
| `models_index.json` | For each model: its ID, its file, what it is for, and why it is not in a chapter |
| `reference/` | Three reference documents: H (a model of typed events, records and authorities), I (a protocol for the evidence of interfaces) and J (control gates, exception patterns and time) |
| `registers/` | Six registers in CSV form: authorities, events, records, control rules, interfaces, and the evidence of interfaces |

A research model is a proposal, a method or a general model. It is not a description of Philippine PFM from a source,
so it is kept apart from the chapters. The column `chapter` of the register of events names the chapter of the atlas
that describes each event.
