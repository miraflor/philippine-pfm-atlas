## 2. The public portal

The COMPASS portal of DBM, launched on 26 June 2026, shows budget information by department, agency, region and purpose [COMPASS-LAUNCH]. Executive Order No. 126 of 28 September 2026 requires national agencies and government corporations to publish through it the financial performance and the implementation status of programs and projects. The order makes each agency responsible "for the accuracy, completeness, and timeliness of the information it submits" [EO126]. The portal therefore shows what agencies report. The documents that I read describe no check of this information by another body before publication. Chapter IS, section 11, gives what is known about the portal and the order.
