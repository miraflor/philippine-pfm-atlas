# Reference D. Legal framework

*Part of the Philippine PFM Atlas. Facts on current events as of 8 October 2026. The codes in the column "Read?" are explained in [Reference F](F_sources_and_verification.md). "Not opened" means that I know the document only from general knowledge or from another document that cites it.*

## 1. Why the rules are in layers

Philippine PFM has no single finance law. The rules are in five layers, and a lower layer may not contradict a higher one.

1. The Constitution fixes who may do what: who proposes the budget, who may change it, who audits.
2. Laws that stay in force for many years fix the general process: the Administrative Code, the Auditing Code, the Local Government Code, the procurement law.
3. The General Appropriations Act of each year fixes the amounts, and its general and special provisions add rules for that year only.
4. Executive orders of the President set policy inside the Executive, for example cash budgeting or the adoption of an integrated system.
5. Circulars of DBM, COA, DOF, BTr and GPPB give the working detail: forms, deadlines, steps.

A consequence for anyone who maps the process: many details (validity of appropriations, release documents, reports) are in layers 3 and 5 and change from year to year. Each chapter of this atlas therefore says for which fiscal year a rule was read.
